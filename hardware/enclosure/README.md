# Enclosure

[esp32quranenclosure.3mf](esp32quranenclosure.3mf) is the enclosure project supplied by the project owner, unchanged. It contains four named parts: `topcover.stl`, `bottom final (1).stl`, `powerbutton.stl`, and `buttonboot.stl`.

Open the 3MF as a project in Bambu Studio to retain the layout and settings. The saved profile is Bambu Lab H2S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.24 mm layers, two walls and 15% infill. These are embedded settings, not independently validated print recommendations. Select your actual printer and filament, review supports and orientation, then slice before printing. Do not send this profile directly to a different printer.

Dry-fit the board and button pieces before assembly. Keep the display free from pressure and confirm USB, SD, slider and power-button access. Verify battery clearances and polarity. Fastener sizes and printed fit have not been independently verified.

The 3MF metadata does not specify a designer or license. CAD is excluded from the software GPL license; confirm authorship and redistribution terms before making the repository public.
